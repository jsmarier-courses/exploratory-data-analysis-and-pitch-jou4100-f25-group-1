## Template for the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Pitch


The data that we used comes from the ‘long for census - ward data,’ sent out by the city of Ottawa in 2021. Our story creation method was to each browse through and find a story we liked and then decide as a group which story to go with. Our key observations varied as we all focused on different sections of the dataset, but we remarked on the fact that ~ 1500 citizens of Ottawa marked down that they speak no French or English which we thought was pretty remarkable.
