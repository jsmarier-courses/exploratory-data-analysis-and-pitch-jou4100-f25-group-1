## Template for the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Pitch

For our story, we wanted to focus on the language statistics and particularly wards that speak the most French and what factors affect these numbers. We used the dataset “2021 Long Form Census - Data” provided by the City of Ottawa. Before diving deeper into doing data analysis, we had to assess the level of correctness of the data, then clean it. After these preliminary steps are finalized, our team observed trends by generating charts. The first thing we did was to find the wards with the top 3 highest percentages of French-speaking population.  By percentage, wards 12 (4.26%), 13 (4.17%), and 19 (3.41%) had the most monolingual French speakers.
